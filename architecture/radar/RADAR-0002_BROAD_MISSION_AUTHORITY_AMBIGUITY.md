# RADAR-0002 — Broad Mission / Authority Ambiguity

Date: 2026-08-12
Subject: PX00 north-star mission
Horizon: H5_GENERALIZED_PRODUCTION
Reversibility: PRACTICALLY_IRREVERSIBLE
Lock-in risk: MEDIUM
Blast radius: PHYSICAL_EXTERNAL
Maturity trigger: NOW
Recommendation: DEFER_WITH_GUARD

## Assumption
A broad mission such as producing what is "needed by humanity" can safely be converted directly into operational objectives.

## Failure mode
An optimization system could infer goals, beneficiaries or trade-offs beyond explicit authorization, creating unsafe, unlawful, unwanted or externally harmful actions while believing it is serving the mission.

## Treatment
Keep the broad statement as North Star only. Every executable objective must identify authorized goal scope, beneficiary/customer, applicable jurisdiction/policy, safety constraints, resources, externalities, acceptance criteria and authority ceiling.

## Guard
Broad aspiration never expands runtime authority. Physical/external actions require narrower domain-specific authorization and safety review.
