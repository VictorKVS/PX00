# ADR-0050 — Factory Builder Minimum Team and Blueprint Synthesis Protocol

Date: 2026-08-12
Status: accepted
Project: PROJECT-FFB-0001

## Context
The first five architecture roles cover structural, organizational, knowledge and security/risk concerns but do not yet guarantee implementability, measurable quality or technology-independent capability decomposition. A factory-design organization also needs an explicit collaboration sequence so specialist opinions do not become an informal meeting process.

## Decision
Add:
- FFB-ROLE-0006 Principal Software Engineer;
- FFB-ROLE-0007 Quality and Assurance Architect;
- FFB-ROLE-0008 Capability Architect.

Adopt `PROTO-FFB-0001 Factory Blueprint Synthesis` as the default design path from authorized need to blueprint handoff.

## Protocol order
1. Requirement qualification.
2. Capability decomposition.
3. Parallel specialist design.
4. Architectural tension registration.
5. Chief Architect synthesis.
6. Implementability review.
7. Quality and acceptance architecture.
8. Security/risk gate.
9. Socrates challenge.
10. ARGUS audit.
11. Rework or summit submission.
12. Governed handoff to FATHER.

## Invariants
- capability precedes executor/model/tool selection;
- incompatible specialist positions are preserved as tensions, not silently averaged;
- untestable material architecture claims may be marked UNPROVEN;
- no PASS without declared criteria and evidence;
- unresolved S4 blocks the affected maturity path;
- producer chain cannot substitute for independent Socrates/ARGUS review;
- blueprint handoff grants no runtime authority.

## Consequence
Factory Builder now has a minimum design team and a formal method for producing its first auditable Agent Factory Blueprint. The next generation should define machine-readable factory requirement/capability/blueprint contracts and execute a synthetic dry-run.
