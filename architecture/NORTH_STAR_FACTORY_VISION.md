# PX00 / FATHER — North-Star Factory Vision

Date: 2026-08-12
Status: strategic direction

## Mission
PX00/FATHER is not ultimately an AI-agent factory. It is a governance and management operating system for building and operating useful, auditable, safe and progressively more autonomous production organizations.

The long-term objective is to make it possible to assemble governed organizations that can research, design, test, produce and improve useful products and services for willing/authorized beneficiaries across different domains and jurisdictions.

## Capability horizons

### H1 — Agent Factory
Build, staff, govern, audit and improve digital roles performed by replaceable AI/software executors.

### H2 — Software and Service Factory
Compose governed digital departments capable of producing software, analysis, security work, documentation, operations and other digital outputs end-to-end.

### H3 — Research Factory
Manage hypotheses, literature/evidence, experiments, simulation, laboratory workflows, reproducibility, independent review and scientific decision gates.

### H4 — Robotic / Cyber-Physical Factory
Bind roles and protocols to robots, sensors, laboratory instruments, industrial controllers, machinery and logistics while preserving authority, safety, traceability and human override.

### H5 — Generalized Production Organization
Coordinate mixed human, AI, software and physical executors under explicit goals, law/policy, resource constraints, quality gates, safety envelopes and measurable outcomes.

## Architectural doctrine
The stable abstraction is not MODEL or ROBOT. The stable abstraction is governed capability.

```text
GOAL
  -> PROJECT
  -> PLAN
  -> TASK
  -> RESPONSIBILITY
  -> ROLE
  -> CAPABILITY REQUIREMENT
  -> ELIGIBLE EXECUTOR
       - human
       - LLM/agent
       - software service
       - simulator
       - laboratory instrument
       - robot
       - industrial machine
  -> AUTHORITY / SAFETY ENVELOPE
  -> EXECUTION
  -> RESULT / EVIDENCE
  -> REVIEW / ACCEPTANCE
```

`ROLE != EXECUTOR != MODEL != DEVICE`.

A future executor may be replaced without changing the organizational responsibility, protocol, knowledge binding, historical evidence or canonical task identity.

## Long-horizon invariants
1. Reliability before speed on critical boundaries.
2. No irreversible material action without explicit authority and applicable safety gates.
3. Simulation/sandbox before real-world side effects when technically meaningful.
4. Human emergency stop/override for safety-critical physical systems unless an explicitly governed domain-specific exception is proven and authorized.
5. Evidence and measurement must remain distinguishable from model opinion.
6. Physical production requires metrology, calibration, maintenance and quality lineage in addition to software traceability.
7. Resources are first-class: money, energy, time, materials, compute, equipment capacity and human attention.
8. Supply-chain provenance and dependency risk are first-class.
9. Jurisdiction, safety, environmental and sector regulation constrain plans before execution.
10. Failure, near-miss and incident history become institutional memory and feed future planning.
11. No KPI may become an optimization target without anti-Goodhart review.
12. No single model/provider/device/vendor becomes the identity of the organization.
13. High-impact systems require independent review and explicit risk acceptance boundaries.
14. Production maturity is gated by evidence, not feature count.

## Future architectural domains implied by the vision
- capability registry and executor abstraction;
- resource/cost/energy/material accounting;
- safety envelope and emergency-stop contracts;
- digital twin / simulation / staged validation;
- physical asset registry, calibration and maintenance;
- experiment and research lifecycle;
- supply-chain and vendor provenance;
- quality management and metrology;
- legal/jurisdiction/environmental policy profiles;
- human-machine coordination;
- incident/near-miss learning;
- production scheduling and optimization under governed constraints.

## Boundary of purpose
PX00 does not define a single universal notion of what humanity "needs". Need, benefit, consent, safety, legality, externalities and distribution are separate governed questions. FATHER may optimize only within an explicitly authorized goal and policy envelope; it must not silently turn a broad aspiration into unlimited authority.

## Current position
The current stage is H1: Agent Factory. The architecture must be built so H2-H5 can be added by introducing new executor/capability/resource/safety adapters rather than rewriting the management core.
