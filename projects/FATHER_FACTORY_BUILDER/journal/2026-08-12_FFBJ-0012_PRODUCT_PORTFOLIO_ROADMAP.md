# FFBJ-0012 — Product Portfolio Roadmap

Date: 2026-08-12
Project: PROJECT-FFB-0001
Related: TF-0065

## Decision
Factory Builder development is now governed by a product portfolio rather than by architecture growth alone.

Primary owner-priority product line:
`Security Knowledge Base → Compliance Engine → Evidence-Based Security Architect → Vulnerability & Risk Expert → White-Hat / Continuous Security Assurance → Regulatory Change Engine → Continuous Security Audit Expert`.

## Portfolio principle
Later products may be explored as bounded PoCs, but foundational product gates may not be bypassed by schedule pressure or a successful demo.

## Architect-proposed supporting tracks
- FATHER/Factory Builder platform;
- Agent Factory;
- general knowledge infrastructure;
- independent assurance;
- Software/Service Factory;
- Research Factory;
- Cyber-Physical/Robotics Factory;
- generalized Factory-of-Factories.

These tracks must not displace the current owner-priority Security Knowledge foundation except where they are direct dependencies, maturity gates or critical-risk remediation.

## Factory Builder consequence
The Factory Builder must optimize for the narrowest useful product-enabling build, not for the largest possible generic platform.

Every proposed architecture generation should answer:
1. Which active product or hard gate does this enable?
2. What evidence will prove the benefit?
3. What risk does it introduce or retire?
4. Can it be deferred without blocking the current product?

## Maturity impact
The project now has an explicit bridge from architecture work to product manufacturing and future domain/factory expansion.
