# FATHER Factory Builder

Project ID: `PROJECT-FFB-0001`
Status: internal incubated project inside PX00
Date: 2026-08-12

## Purpose
`FATHER Factory Builder` is the internal meta-project that designs, assembles, validates and progressively matures governed factories/organizations for a declared need.

It is intentionally separated from the FATHER management runtime.

- **FATHER** manages an operating organization.
- **Factory Builder** designs and erects the organization/factory that FATHER will later manage.
- **ARGUS** independently challenges architecture, risks and maturity claims.
- **Socrates/reviewers** independently challenge work products and evidence.

The project begins inside PX00 so it can reuse governance contracts and evolve quickly. It is designed for later extraction into one or more repositories without changing stable IDs or historical lineage.

## What is a factory?
A factory is any governed production organization capable of repeatedly turning authorized needs and resources into accepted outputs.

Examples:
- AI-agent factory;
- software/service factory;
- research factory;
- security/OSINT organization;
- simulation/design factory;
- robotic/cyber-physical factory;
- mixed human/AI/software/physical production organization.

## Core rule
A factory is never accepted because it exists. It is accepted because its intended outcomes, authority boundaries, safety properties, quality criteria, evidence, recovery strategy and known residual risks are appropriate for the declared maturity level.
