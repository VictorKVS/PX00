# Factory Builder — Role and Knowledge Design Doctrine

Date: 2026-08-12
Status: active doctrine
Project: `PROJECT-FFB-0001`

## Principle
A factory is not assembled from models. It is assembled from governed responsibilities and capabilities. Each stable organizational role is designed independently of the current worker/model/device assigned to it.

`ROLE != AGENT != MODEL != DEVICE != KNOWLEDGE_SPACE`.

## Mandatory Role Package
Every material role must define at minimum:
1. stable `role_id` and display name;
2. mission/purpose;
3. department and reporting line;
4. responsibilities and explicit non-responsibilities;
5. required capabilities;
6. decision rights and authority ceiling;
7. prohibited actions;
8. escalation paths;
9. handoff inputs and outputs;
10. protocols the role may execute;
11. evidence/acceptance obligations;
12. independence/conflict-of-interest constraints;
13. knowledge bindings;
14. data/classification boundaries;
15. tool/capability requirements without direct grants;
16. performance and quality measures;
17. anti-Goodhart controls;
18. failure modes and known risks;
19. continuity/deputy/replacement rules;
20. review cadence and version history.

## Knowledge design
Knowledge is organizational infrastructure, not private agent memory.

Each role is bound to one or more stable logical knowledge spaces, for example:
- `KB-ARCHITECTURE`
- `KB-SECURITY`
- `KB-PROGRAMMING`
- `KB-DEVSECOPS`
- `KB-PRODUCT`
- `KB-RESEARCH`
- `KB-OSINT`
- `KB-AI-AGENTS`
- future domain-specific spaces such as `KB-ROBOTICS`, `KB-METROLOGY`, `KB-MANUFACTURING`, `KB-SAFETY`, `KB-SUPPLY-CHAIN`.

A role may use several spaces and one space may serve several roles. The binding must define protocol, object types, classification ceiling and freshness requirements. Binding knowledge never grants runtime authority.

## Physical repository strategy
At incubation, multiple logical spaces may live in `KNOWLEDGE_CORE`. As domains grow they may be extracted to dedicated repositories. Physical extraction must preserve:
- logical `knowledge_space_id`;
- object IDs and provenance;
- graph relationships;
- historical route versions;
- role bindings;
- audit/replay references.

`LOGICAL IDENTITY != PHYSICAL LOCATION`.

## When a role deserves a dedicated knowledge base
A separate logical knowledge domain is justified when one or more conditions apply:
- the role needs a specialized corpus and vocabulary;
- evidence/provenance rules differ materially;
- freshness/update cadence differs;
- access/classification differs;
- the domain has independent maintainers/reviewers;
- the corpus is large enough that shared retrieval harms precision;
- the domain needs its own validation, benchmarks or regulatory mapping;
- extraction will reduce coupling without duplicating canonical knowledge.

## Knowledge quality
Each role-specific KB must distinguish:
- normative sources;
- primary evidence;
- secondary analysis;
- patterns/guides;
- experiments/tests;
- lessons/incidents/near-misses;
- deprecated/superseded material.

No unverified model output becomes authoritative knowledge merely because it was generated by a role.

## Role creation gate
Factory Builder may create a new role only if:
- a stable responsibility exists;
- the responsibility cannot be cleanly assigned to an existing role;
- inputs/outputs and handoffs are identifiable;
- authority can be bounded;
- acceptance can be measured;
- required knowledge can be governed;
- duplication/organizational complexity is justified.

Otherwise prefer extending/refining an existing role.

## Long-term objective
Role Packages and logical knowledge domains become reusable factory components. Factory Builder should eventually be able to assemble a new organization by selecting proven role/capability/knowledge modules, adapting them under governed profiles, and sending the resulting blueprint to FATHER for operation.
