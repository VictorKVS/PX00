# Factory Builder Roadmap

The roadmap separates capability horizons from maturity. A factory type may be explored early, but promotion is governed by evidence and risk gates.

## Phase FFB-0 — Internal incubation (now)
Goal: define the Factory Builder doctrine inside PX00 while FATHER itself is still being built.

Deliverables:
- project charter;
- design/implementation principles;
- construction lifecycle;
- risk/failure atlas;
- discovery/learning loop;
- extraction boundary.

Exit criteria:
- project can be reasoned about independently from FATHER runtime;
- stable project identity and interfaces exist;
- future extraction does not require renaming core concepts.

## Phase FFB-1 — Agent Factory Builder
Goal: Factory Builder can design an AI/software organization from a declared goal.

It must generate/validate:
- capability map;
- departments/roles/responsibilities;
- protocols and handoffs;
- knowledge bindings;
- executor eligibility;
- review/audit topology;
- risk matrix;
- acceptance/maturity gates.

First demonstrator: build a small governed multi-agent factory and hand it to FATHER for operation.

## Phase FFB-2 — Digital Service Factory Builder
Goal: design factories producing complete digital services/products, not isolated agent tasks.

Add:
- service/product lifecycle;
- software delivery/operations;
- cost/resource accounting;
- durable persistence and SRE/operations concerns;
- external integrations and customer/service boundaries.

## Phase FFB-3 — Research Factory Builder
Goal: design repeatable research organizations.

Add:
- hypothesis/experiment contracts;
- literature/evidence synthesis;
- simulation and experimental planning;
- reproducibility;
- lab notebook/provenance;
- independent scientific review;
- negative-result memory.

## Phase FFB-4 — Cyber-Physical Factory Builder
Goal: design factories that safely coordinate physical executors.

Add before live action:
- asset/device registry;
- safety envelope;
- hazard analysis;
- digital twin/simulation;
- interlocks/emergency stop;
- metrology/calibration;
- maintenance;
- staged commissioning;
- cyber-physical incident response.

## Phase FFB-5 — Generalized Production Factory Builder
Goal: mixed human + AI + software + robot + equipment organizations.

Add:
- materials/energy/capacity planning;
- procurement/supply chain;
- production scheduling;
- quality/metrology system;
- environmental/externality controls;
- multi-factory coordination;
- lifecycle optimization under governed constraints.

## Phase FFB-6 — Factory portfolio / factory-of-factories
Goal: manage a portfolio of specialized factories and decide when to create, reuse, merge, split, retire or rebuild them.

Critical rule: Factory Builder may recommend factory creation/change, but high-impact creation and authority expansion remain governed decisions.

# Current position
`FFB-0 Internal incubation` while PX00 is at H1 Agent Factory foundation.

# Next operational milestone
`SUMMIT-FFB-01 — Agent Factory Blueprint`:
Given one bounded goal, Factory Builder produces a reviewable complete organization blueprint with roles, responsibilities, knowledge, protocols, executor requirements, risk register, acceptance criteria and maturity plan — without executing the factory itself.
