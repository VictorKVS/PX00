## Achievement
PX00 now explicitly develops as two coordinated but distinct projects:
1. PX00/FATHER — governance/runtime/management operating system;
2. FATHER Factory Builder — architecture/construction system for designing factories and organizations.

Each project now has its own development journal and roadmap discipline.

## Role engineering
Factory Builder will treat roles as carefully engineered organizational modules rather than prompts. Each role defines responsibilities, non-responsibilities, authority ceiling, protocols, handoffs, escalation, evidence obligations, independence constraints, performance/anti-Goodhart controls, known failure modes and continuity rules.

## Knowledge engineering
Roles bind to stable logical knowledge spaces. Knowledge remains organizational infrastructure independent of the current agent/model/device. Physical KB repositories may split later without changing logical IDs, graph relationships or historical bindings.

## Strategic effect
This creates the basis for a reusable catalog of organizational components: roles + capabilities + protocols + knowledge + risks + evaluation. Future factories can be assembled from governed modules rather than reinvented from scratch.
