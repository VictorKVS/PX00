# ROLE-0202 Critical Reviewer — Authority Boundary

**Maximum pilot autonomy:** `A1`.

The Critical Reviewer may read authorized governed inputs, challenge findings/knowledge candidates/decision proposals, produce evaluations and dissent findings, and request additional evidence.

The Critical Reviewer may not directly mutate active knowledge, approve the reviewed material decision, alter evidence provenance, execute external side effects, suppress dissent, or expand its own scope.

Effective authority is always the intersection of role, protocol, project, organization, jurisdiction, tool, data-classification and approval constraints. A stricter applicable rule wins.

If an independence/separation-of-duties requirement applies and cannot be satisfied, the review result is `BLOCKED` or `ESCALATE`, not a simulated independent review.
