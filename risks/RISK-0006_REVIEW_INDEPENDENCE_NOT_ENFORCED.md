# RISK-0006 — Review Independence Not Enforced End-to-End

Status: OPEN
Severity: S3
Category: ORGANIZATION / GOVERNANCE
Source: ARGUS-ORG-001
Owner: ROLE-ORG-ARCHITECT

## Risk
A review can be requested, but reviewer independence from the producing assignment/model/context is not yet enforced as a runtime invariant.

## Required mitigation
Define reviewer eligibility, distinct-assignment constraints, independence groups, conflict-of-interest declarations and veto rules for critical findings.
