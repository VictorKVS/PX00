# TF-0052 — North-Star Capability Factory

Date: 2026-08-12
Status: strategic architecture accepted
ADR: ADR-0046

## Generation
Expanded the long-term identity of PX00/FATHER from an AI-agent factory to a governed capability/production organization architecture.

## Horizons
H1 Agent Factory -> H2 Software/Service Factory -> H3 Research Factory -> H4 Robotic/Cyber-Physical Factory -> H5 Generalized Production Organization.

## Core abstraction
`ROLE != EXECUTOR != MODEL != DEVICE`.
Tasks require capabilities; governed executor adapters may later bind those capabilities to people, agents, software, simulators, instruments, robots or industrial machines.

## New long-horizon concerns identified
- resource/cost/energy/material accounting;
- digital twins and simulation-before-action;
- physical safety envelopes and emergency stop;
- calibration/metrology/maintenance;
- experiment/research lifecycle;
- supply-chain provenance;
- environmental/jurisdictional constraints;
- incident and near-miss learning;
- production scheduling and constrained optimization.

## Current stage
H1 — Agent Factory. Future horizons must extend rather than replace the management core.
